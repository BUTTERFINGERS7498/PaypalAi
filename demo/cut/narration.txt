01|AI agents can now spend your money. But when a charge is disputed, who can prove what the agent was allowed to do? That is the problem Sentry solves.
02|Picture a helper robot doing your shopping. Sentry gives it a signed permission slip, a strict checker, and a notebook that cannot be quietly changed.
03|Let us see it work. First, the mandate: a signed rulebook for the agent.
04|Two hundred dollars a month, groceries only, and anything above seventy-five dollars waits for your approval.
05|Every mandate is signed, and one click checks the signature.
06|Now we tell the agent what to buy, in plain English.
07|It picks an item, the checker confirms it fits the mandate, and a PayPal sandbox order is created.
08|Every choice lands in the ledger, with its mandate check beside it.
09|Now the honest part. A clearly labelled demo switch lets the agent break its own rules.
10|The record still tells the truth: wrong category, over the cap.
11|Every decision is written down: what the agent saw, what it chose, and the fingerprint of the record before it.
12|One click checks the whole chain.
13|When a charge is disputed, Sentry builds the response from this evidence, and code decides the verdict. First, the rogue purchase.
14|Mandate breach. Sentry says accept the claim, instead of defending a purchase it should never have allowed.
15|And when the agent behaved, the same process gives a defensible verdict: provide the evidence, and the record proves it.
16|Sentry. Signed mandates, tamper-evident evidence, and honest dispute answers, on the PayPal sandbox.
